Current Location

Area of Expertise

Urban Design Public Policy Equity Advocacy Civic Infrastructure

John Doe is a visionary leader in urban design with over two decades of experience shaping public spaces in New York City. His work focuses on intersectional equity and sustainable infrastructure, ensuring that the city's architectural evolution serves all its citizens.

As the Executive Director, he oversees the design review process for all permanent structures, landscapes, and artworks on City-owned property. His tenure has been marked by a radical commitment to community-driven design and the preservation of cultural heritage in rapidly developing neighborhoods.

The Interview

What sparked your interest in urban design?

"Growing up in Brooklyn, I saw how the physical environment dictated the opportunities available to people. I realized that the way a park is built or how a transit line is drawn isn't just a technical decision—it's a moral one."

How do you define 'equity' in public design?

It means that quality design is not a luxury, but a right. It's about dismantling the invisible barriers that keep certain communities from feeling welcome in public spaces. We ask: Who is this for? Who was left out of the conversation? And how do we fix it?
