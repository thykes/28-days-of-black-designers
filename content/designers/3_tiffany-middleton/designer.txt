Title: From the Baseline to Bristol: The Vision of Tiffany Middleton

----

Text: Growing up in a small town in Alabama, design was an unknown entity to me. Oddly, I always found myself admiring cover art and layouts in the magazine sections of local grocery stores. As fate would have it, the term "graphic design" was introduced to me via an issue of Teen Vogue. Years later, I graduated from Auburn University with a degree in graphic design—a journey that would take me from the field of Jordan-Hare Stadium to a trading card company outside of Dallas, Texas, and finally to Bristol, Connecticut: the home of ESPN.

  type: qa
  question: >
    What advice would you give to those from
    similar backgrounds?
  answer: >
    It is not always about what you know,
    but who you know. The design world can
    be surprisingly small. Making
    connections through social media and
    networking events is vital to getting
    your foot in the door. You may produce
    great work, but if no one knows your
    brand, it is hard to break in. Use
    social media as a tool to get eyes on
    your work and don’t be afraid to ask
    for feedback. Many Creative
    Directors—including my own—use
    social media to find and hire new
    talent.
