Intro:

Jermaine T. Bell is a visual designer who holds a B.F.A. in Graphic Design from the Maryland Institute College of Art (MICA). Through his work at Havas Worldwide, Bell gained footing in advertising with clients like Michelob Ultra, Liberty Mutual Insurance, and Constellation Energy.   

In 2016, Bell wrapped his tenure as one of Fagan Harris' Baltimore Corps fellows, where he served as the Marketing & Programming Director for Impact Hub Baltimore (IHB). There, he developed brand identity and assets through graphic design support, managed social media, and formulated messaging and outreach plans for diverse communities.   

He created the Rise Residency with author D. Watkins to highlight thought leadership in inclusion and equity. He also organized and executed all public Impact Hub Baltimore events, curated ongoing art exhibitions, and developed event structure and strategy to ensure registration through cross-promotion and partnerships.

**Currently Building**
Building on his work at Impact Hub Baltimore, Bell is now an Open Society Fellow where he hopes to engage the communities around ExitTheApple, located in the Barclay neighborhood.

"I hope to create a system that supports Black artists and allows their ideas to grow and thrive. I am excited about my partnership with ExitTheApple, which already has an established reputation within the community."

In 2015, as Programming Chair of AIGA, Bell spearheaded REdesign & REbuild, an initiative that partnered with The Neighborhood Design Center to rebrand and relaunch marketing collateral for small businesses after the Baltimore unrest of April 2015. The Druid Heights Community Development Corporation was the first recipient of this work.

Bell is also a budding entrepreneur who sells handcrafted prints and stationery featuring designs inspired by the African diaspora.
