Hero-headline: Honoring the architects of visual culture.

----

Hero-subheadline: A curated celebration of Black excellence in design, spotlighting one visionary creator every day of February.

----

Mission-quote: This project isn't just about a single month. It's about building a permanent archive of talent that has historically been overlooked, ensuring the next generation of designers sees themselves reflected in the industry's history.

----

Founder-name: Tim Hykes

----

Founder-role: Creator · Designer

----

Founder-bio: <p>Tim Hykes is a Senior Interaction Designer at Google, designing AI initiatives in Search. He started the <strong>28 Days of Black Designers Project</strong> in 2007 as a personal project to track the incredible talent he discovered across the industry. A former President of AIGA Saint Louis, Tim focuses on the intersection of generative AI and design, ensuring global products are built for eveyone.</p>
